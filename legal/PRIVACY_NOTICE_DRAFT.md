## 1. What this service does

[App name] turns Afrikaans speech into text. By default it runs entirely on your own device: the recording and the transcript stay on your computer or phone, and nothing is sent to us.

## 2. Information we process

| When | What | Where it is processed | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local transcription (default) | Audio and transcripts | Your device only | Under your control; the app can auto-delete recordings after a period you choose |
| Cloud fallback (opt-in) | The audio file you choose to send | Our processing provider in [country/region] | Audio deleted within 48 hours of transcription; transcript returned to your device and not retained by us |
| Data donation (opt-in, per recording) | Audio and your corrected transcript | Our secure storage | Until you withdraw, or [24 months], whichever is first |
| Account (if you sign in) | Email address, plan, usage counts | Our database provider | While the account exists |
| App diagnostics | Crash reports without audio or transcript content | Our error-reporting provider | 90 days |

## 3. Why we process it and on what basis

- To provide the transcription you asked for (performance of our agreement with you).
- To improve Afrikaans speech recognition, only with your explicit consent for each donated recording.
- Voice recordings may be regarded as special personal information under POPIA. We rely on your explicit consent for cloud processing and for data donation.

## 4. Recordings of other people

You are responsible for making sure you may record a conversation. Under RICA a participant in a conversation may record it. The app shows a recording indicator and gives you a message you can send to participants. If you donate a recording, you confirm that the participants agreed.

## 5. Transfers outside South Africa

Local transcription involves no transfer. Cloud fallback and data donation may involve processing by a provider outside South Africa under a written data-processing agreement that requires protection substantially similar to POPIA. We ask for your consent before either is switched on.

## 6. Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold, ask for correction or deletion, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to the Information Regulator ([contact]). Use the "Delete my data" function in the app or email the Information Officer.

## 7. Security

Transfers are encrypted in transit; cloud audio is stored encrypted and deleted on a schedule; access is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at [email] if you believe your information has been compromised.

## 8. Changes

We will post updates here and in the app.
