# PAIA manual outline

The Promotion of Access to Information Act requires a manual from every private body. The Information Regulator publishes a template; this outline lists what to fill in. Draft from the Regulator's current template rather than from memory.

1. Contact details of the body and the Information Officer (and deputy, if any).
2. Guide published by the Information Regulator on how to use PAIA (reference and where to obtain it).
3. Categories of records held: user accounts, donated recordings and transcripts, consent register, supplier contracts, financial records, software and documentation.
4. Records automatically available without a request: this manual, the privacy notice, the website.
5. Records available under other legislation (for example company and tax records).
6. How to request access: the prescribed form, fees, and the Information Officer's address.
7. Processing of personal information (POPIA section): purpose of processing, categories of data subjects and information, recipients, cross-border transfers, security measures.
8. Availability of the manual: on the website and on request.
9. Date of last update and signature of the Information Officer.
